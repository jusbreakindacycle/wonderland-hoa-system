# Wonderland Homeowners Association, Inc. — Formal Adoption Action List

## Purpose

This is the short officer-facing action list derived from the approved Phase 1 Policy, Governance and Controls Register. It identifies what should be documented or formally adopted before the future system enforces it as association policy.

## Priority A — Financial and Membership Rules

1. Confirm the registered legal name using the registration certificate or bylaws.
2. Locate or adopt the governing record for ₱400 monthly dues per property.
3. Record the effective date of the increase from ₱300 to ₱400.
4. Confirm in writing that there is no formal monthly due date and no late-payment fine.
5. Adopt the vacant-property definition, evidence, approval path, effective month and reoccupation process.
6. Adopt oldest-unpaid-balance-first as the default payment-allocation rule.
7. Adopt automatic application of overpayment credit to the next month’s property due.
8. Adopt the delinquency/not-in-good-standing and vehicle-sticker eligibility process, including notice and reconsideration/appeal.

## Priority B — Receipt and Financial Controls

1. Confirm the physical receipt format and whether a carbon/duplicate office copy exists.
2. Establish one sequential receipt register covering dues, sports fines and other association receipts.
3. Adopt receipt statuses: Issued, Cancelled, Voided and Replaced.
4. Require reasons, actor, approver, date and replacement linkage.
5. Prohibit deletion or reuse of receipt numbers.
6. Adopt separate ledger categories for dues and sports fines.
7. Adopt a refund workflow with Treasurer preparation, second approval and Auditor review.
8. Reserve waivers and write-offs for Board approval.

## Priority C — Governance and Roles

1. Record current Board, officer and committee start/end dates and appointment/election basis.
2. Adopt a separation-of-duties matrix.
3. Confirm which financial corrections may be delegated and which require Board resolution.
4. Confirm that dues changes require the member approval/ratification required by the bylaws and applicable law.
5. Establish the Auditor as independent reviewer rather than sole approver of adjustments.
6. Adopt a Board-review process for long-term or indefinite guest restrictions.

## Priority D — Records and Information

1. Establish the Secretary as principal custodian of the master property list and governance archive.
2. Create an index of bylaws, resolutions, minutes, memoranda, notices, forms and superseded rules.
3. Adopt document issue date, effective date, version and superseded-status requirements.
4. Establish a permanent resident Knowledge Base linked to source documents.
5. Confirm retention periods for receipts, permits, visitor records, violation records and financial records.

## Priority E — Account Provisioning

1. Register properties and verified owner relationships before resident onboarding.
2. Use one personal account per person, linked to all authorised properties.
3. Do not create default, property or shared login accounts.
4. Use one-time invitations or activation links.
5. Adopt access revocation, password reset and relationship-history procedures.

## Formalisation Note

The management system will record and enforce adopted association decisions; it must not be used as the instrument that silently creates those decisions. Board resolutions, member approvals and bylaw amendments must be completed through the association’s valid governance process.
